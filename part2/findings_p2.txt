# SHAP
It can be seen that the features most important are NumHeavyAtoms, MolWt, MinPartialCharge, FractionCSP3, and MaxPartialCharge.
- NumHeavyAtoms and MolWt correlate with molecular size. Larger molecules have a larger pi system and more electron delocalization. This causes 
a smaller HOMO-LUMO Gap as larger conjugated systems have more closely spaced energy levels. Also called particle-in-a-box effect.
- MinPartialCharge and MaxPartialCharge are also imporant as the difference in charges will pull electrons further or closer to the nucleus or site of 
partial charge. This greatly lowers (or increases for MaxPartialCharge) the HOMO. However, because the LUMO is further it will be less affected
by the charge and therefore change less if an electron is in the LUMO.
- FractionCSP3 measure what fraction of carbons are sp3 hybridized. sp3 carbons have no p orbitals available for pi bonding, so fewer pi electrons are available
for conjugation (bonding). Less conjugation means more local electrons and therefore the HOMO-LUMO Gap increases.
This is given by E=n^2h^2/8mL^2 where L is delcalization. Higher L = less energy = smaller gap.
- While not shown, the Random Forests regressor showed similar results with only TPSA being higher. It is unknown why this is as TPSA is a 3d 
descriptor and makes little sense of the 3d HOMO-LUMO Gap. TPSA may be affected by polar groups which could then change HOMO-LUMO Gap. further research required

Ring features such as NumAromaticCarbocycles, NumAromaticHeterocycles, and NumAromaticRings all appear to be at the bottom.
- This makes sense as the data already has bee processed to have at least 1 Aromatic ring and so the affect of this will be 
diminished as the columns likely consist of mostly 1 or 2.
